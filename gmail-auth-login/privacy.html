Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

About Login Tester

Login Tester is an independent demonstration application used to test Google Sign-In. It is not affiliated with Google.

Information received

After you choose to sign in, Google Identity Services returns basic identity information, which may include your name, email address, profile photo, email verification status, and Google account subject ID.

How information is used

The information is used only to demonstrate authentication and display your profile details in your browser. Login Tester does not read, send, modify, or delete Gmail messages and does not request Gmail mailbox scopes.

Storage and sharing

Login Tester does not send or store your Google profile information on an application server. The information is not sold or shared by Login Tester. The Google Identity Services client may use Google's own services as described in Google's policies.

Local browser settings

The optional Google OAuth client ID entered in the configuration panel is stored only in your browser's local storage. You can remove it with the Clear button or by clearing this site's browser data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner of the Login Tester deployment through the contact details provided with the associated Google Cloud project.
